# The Alien Math Archives

**Researcher:** Philippe Beauchamp ([ORCID 0009-0003-7407-394X](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-7407-394X))

A separate archive for exploratory system architectures and unconventional research, with clear boundaries between established mathematics, formal specifications, proposals, and open questions.

## Publication and evidence policy

- **PROVED** — established from stated hypotheses with a complete argument.
- **DERIVED** — follows from documented reasoning under stated assumptions.
- **COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED** — checked by an identified, reproducible computation.
- **PROPOSED** — exploratory concept not yet validated.
- **OPEN** — unresolved or lacking sufficient evidence.

A specification is not proof of implementation. Simulation is not physical experimentation. This repository will not contain private calculations, unpublished proofs, confidential parameters, or other private mathematics unless specifically reviewed and approved for release.
