An organized public archive for unconventional mathematics, computational architectures, and carefully labeled exploratory technology research.

## Evidence and publication policy

This repository distinguishes:
- **PROVED** — follows from stated hypotheses and a complete argument.
- **DERIVED** — follows from documented calculations under stated assumptions.
- **COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED** — reproduced by an identified computational procedure.
- **PROPOSED** — a concept or design awaiting validation.
- **OPEN** — unresolved or lacking sufficient evidence.

A specification is not evidence of a working physical device. Simulations are not physical experiments. Unreleased mathematics, private notes, confidential parameters, and unpublished proofs are not included here.
