# The Alien Math Archives

**Researcher:** Philippe Beauchamp ([ORCID 0009-0003-7407-394X](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-7407-394X))  
**Focus:** Independent mathematical research, computational verification, lattice structures, PDEs, and formal system architectures.

The Alien Math Archives is a navigation point for a portfolio of distinct research programs. It is designed to make the scope of the work visible while keeping published results, derived calculations, formal proposals, and unresolved questions clearly separated.

## Research milestones and public record

These are documented research outputs and milestones—not claims that every open problem below has been solved.

### 1. Hodge / Weil-type abelian fourfolds

A public research release on Weil-type abelian fourfolds, arithmetic realizations, discriminant invariants, and computational certificates. The public package includes a sequence of H01–H12 certificates and explicit lattice calculations, with unresolved geometric-realization questions kept open.

- [Open the Hodge research folder](https://github.com/jackophil-dev/public-math-research/tree/main/HODGE/)

### 6. Reproducibility and research-integrity framework

Across these tracks, the public record distinguishes mathematical derivation from exact computation, arithmetic invariants from geometric realization, and formal system specifications from experimental evidence. Certificates and explicit open-problem registers make it easier for others to inspect what is supported and what still needs work.

### 7. Independent publication and research organization

The work is organized into distinct, inspectable research tracks with public GitHub files and linked Zenodo records. The individual publication records remain the reference for their deposited versions; this archive is a navigation page, not a claim that every folder is a byte-for-byte publication mirror.

## Evidence and publication policy

- **PROVED** — established from stated hypotheses with a complete argument.
- **DERIVED** — follows from documented reasoning under stated assumptions.
- **COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED** — checked by an identified, reproducible computation.
- **PROPOSED** — an exploratory concept not yet validated.
- **OPEN** — unresolved or lacking sufficient evidence.

A specification is not proof of implementation. A simulation is not physical experimentation. No open conjecture is presented here as solved merely because a framework, certificate list, or candidate derivation exists.
